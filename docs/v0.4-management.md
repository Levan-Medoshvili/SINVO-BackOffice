# BackOffice and Cashier v0.4

- **Live checkouts:** choose a branch to see its POS baskets and item-removal forms. The paginated history below includes the item, quantity, reason, administrator and confirmation status. Idle pages refresh every ten seconds; editing a removal reason pauses refresh. Stale, changed or paid baskets remain protected by server-side checks.
- **Removal notices:** X dismisses the Cashier notice immediately. Company settings controls expiry (default 10 minutes, allowed 1–1440). Closing or expiry never deletes audit history. Old notices receive one bounded lifetime on upgrade.
- **POS terminals → Add:** choose branch, name and code. Saving generates a one-use five-character code valid for ten minutes, tied to that terminal. Regenerate an unused code without affecting other terminals. Already paired terminals cannot be silently reassigned.
- **Advertisements:** text and/or PNG, JPEG or WebP images, branch targets, order, duration and optional schedule. Images up to 5 MB/20 megapixels are normalized to static WebP at up to 1600×900. Up to ten active campaigns rotate below the basket on Cashier and customer displays. Verified images are cached offline. Cached end times are respected offline; pauses and changes arrive at the next successful sync (normally within one minute). The company welcome text is the fallback.
- **Cashier updates:** import signed releases, target all or selected branches, schedule, inspect POS versions and rollout status, or cancel. See [cashier-updates.md](cashier-updates.md) for trusted-starter setup.

ERP deployment remains restricted to Production. Migrations 0005/0006 preserve company, POS, sales and removal data. New permissions are `manage_advertisements` and `manage_updates`; administrators have both, other users require an explicit grant. Notice dismissal, advertisement playlists and managed-update polling require Cashier v0.4. Older clients retain their existing checkout behavior.
